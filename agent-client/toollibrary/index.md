# Capability Library Domain Index

- Scientific Search and External Data：Web search, page fetching and close reading, biomedical database and literature search, and code-library documentation lookup.
- General Bioinformatics Workflows and Toolchains：General bioinformatics CLI capabilities: public data fetching, sequence and variant processing, peak calling and enrichment, regulatory and epigenome editing, multi-omics, and workflow orchestration.
- Omics and Single-Cell Analysis：Single-cell transcriptomics, differential expression, NGS signal processing, phylogenetics, public database queries, flow cytometry, and microbial dynamics analysis.
- Imaging and Signal Analysis：Analysis and processing of microscopy images, DICOM and clinical imaging, and Neuropixels electrophysiological recordings.
- Clinical and Medical Applications：Interpretation of medical data and generation of clinical documents: cancer genomics, clinical decision support, clinical reports, and treatment plans.
- Experimental Design and Automation：Molecular cloning, synthetic biology, immunology and pharmacology wet-lab analysis, glycobiology, and Opentrons liquid-handling robot protocols.
- Research Methods and Communication：Framing hypotheses, designing studies and analysis plans, appraising evidence, writing manuscripts, and reviewing research.
- Skill and Tool Development：Meta-capabilities for creating, modifying, and evaluating Agent Skills.
